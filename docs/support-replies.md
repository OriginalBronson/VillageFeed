# Launch-Week Saved Replies (plan 11-D)

You are support, moderation, and ops. Paste-and-personalize these for the five
predictable emails. Keep the moderator build installed and the Supabase
dashboard bookmarked (Auth → Users, Table editor → review_cases, reports, Logs).

## 1. "I can't sign in"

> Sorry about that! Two quick checks: (1) if you signed up with email, tap
> "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen — the reset link reopens the app;
> (2) if you originally used Sign in with Apple or Google, use that same
> button rather than email. If neither works, tell me roughly when you created
> the account and I'll look at the logs.

## 2. "Delete my data"

> You can do this entirely in-app: Profile → Account → Delete account. It
> permanently removes your profile, dishes, photos, messages, and matches from
> our servers immediately. If you can't access the app, reply confirming the
> email on the account and I'll delete it from our side within 48 hours.

## 3. "I reported someone — what happens now?"

> Thank you — reports freeze the profile immediately, so they're already
> hidden from everyone while a human reviews the case (usually within a day).
> You won't hear the outcome (we keep moderation private) but the freeze is
> instant, and blocking is always available if you never want to see them
> regardless of the outcome.

## 4. "Why is my profile frozen?" (appeal)

> Your profile was reported or flagged and is hidden from Discover while it's
> reviewed — usually within a day. Reviews are done by a human. If you think
> this was a mistake or a misuse of reporting, reply with anything you'd like
> me to consider and I'll prioritize the case. [Check review_cases before
> replying: if the report is obviously bogus, resolve → approve first and say
> so.]

## 5. "I want a refund"

> Subscriptions and refunds are handled by Apple directly (we never see your
> payment details). Request one at https://reportaproblem.apple.com — sign in,
> find VillageFeed Plus, choose "Request a refund". Apple usually answers
> within a day or two. If you meant to cancel auto-renewal: Settings → your
> name → Subscriptions → VillageFeed Plus → Cancel.

## Incident report ("this food made me sick") — not a template, a protocol

1. The dish is already paused automatically; the review case is tagged INCIDENT.
2. Reply personally and fast (target < 2 hours): acknowledge, ask about
   symptoms/timing, advise seeing a doctor if serious. Do not speculate about
   cause or admit fault on behalf of the cook.
3. Freeze the cook's profile from the moderator build if the report is
   credible and severe; otherwise leave the dish paused pending their response.
4. Log everything in the review case notes.
