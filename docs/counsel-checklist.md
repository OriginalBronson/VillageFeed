# Counsel Consultation Checklist (plan 09-B)

Bring this list + `terms-of-service.md` (draft) + `privacy-policy.md` to the
food-law/platform-liability consultation. Goal: one focused hour.

## Questions

1. **Recurring organized exchange = "distribution to the public"?**
   Cottage-food / food-freedom laws are state-by-state and mostly regulate
   *sale*. Does a recurring supper-group swap (weekly, organized via an app,
   no money) constitute regulated distribution anywhere we might launch?
   → This answer feeds the launch-metro choice (plan 12-A1).

2. **Good-Samaritan framing.** Federal Bill Emerson Act + state equivalents
   cover food *donations*. Does structuring trades as mutual gifting (which
   they are — Terms §4) meaningfully change platform or user exposure?

3. **Assumption of risk / release enforceability** (Terms §3) in the launch
   state, given in-app acknowledgment + recorded ToS version/timestamp.
   Sufficient, or is a separate signed acknowledgment needed?

4. **Prohibited-foods list** (Terms §5): complete for the launch state?
   Anything to add/remove (e.g., acidified foods, fermented products)?

5. **Arbitration + class-action waiver** (Terms §13): recommended for a
   platform this size? Governing law choice?

6. **Indemnification scope** (Terms §12): should users indemnify the platform
   for claims arising from their trades/content?

7. **Entity formation**: LLC before launch, or acceptable to launch as sole
   proprietor with these Terms? (Liability posture is the whole question.)

8. **Insurance**: platform liability coverage — when does it become
   worth pricing? Note answer in the risk register either way.

9. **Minor-specific exposure**: the app is 17+; is the age gate +
   moderation-rejects-apparent-minors posture adequate?

## Decisions to leave the meeting with

- [ ] Launch state confirmed favorable (or gating switch required)
- [ ] Terms §3, §5, §12, §13 brackets resolved
- [ ] Entity/insurance decision recorded in docs/DECISIONS or README
- [ ] Green light (or conditions) for hosting Terms v1.0 and removing "-draft"
