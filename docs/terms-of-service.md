## 1. What VillageFeed is — and is not

VillageFeed is a **venue**: an app that helps neighbors find each other to
arrange private, non-commercial exchanges of home-cooked food ("trades").

**We do not prepare, handle, inspect, store, transport, or guarantee any food.**
We are not a food establishment, caterer, restaurant, food broker, or delivery
service. Every trade is a private arrangement between users, made entirely at
their own judgment and risk. We are not a party to any trade.

## 2. Eligibility

You must be **17 years of age or older** to use the Service. By creating an
account you represent that you meet this requirement. We may suspend accounts
that appear to belong to minors.

## 3. Assumption of risk and release

Home kitchens are **not inspected or licensed**. Food traded through the
Service is prepared by private individuals in uninspected home kitchens, and
may present risks including foodborne illness, undeclared allergens, and
cross-contamination.

**By using the Service you knowingly and voluntarily assume all risks arising
from preparing, exchanging, and consuming home-prepared food**, and you release
us, to the maximum extent permitted by law, from any claim arising out of any
trade, including illness, allergic reaction, injury, or property damage, and
from the acts or omissions of other users, both online and at in-person
handoffs.

Meeting people you met through the Service is at your own risk. We encourage —
and the app is designed around — handoffs at **public locations**.

## 4. Strictly non-commercial

Trades are **gifts exchanged between neighbors**. On the Service you may not:

- sell food, or solicit or accept money, gift cards, or anything of monetary
  value for food (including "suggested donations");
- advertise a food business, catering, meal-prep service, or delivery service;
- use the Service to source customers for any commercial activity.

Selling home-prepared food triggers food-service licensing laws. Violations of
this section are grounds for immediate account termination.

## 5. Prohibited foods

The following may **not** be offered or traded on the Service, because they
carry elevated risk and are restricted under many state cottage-food and
food-safety regimes even outside commercial sale:

- **Raw (unpasteurized) milk** and products made from it
- **Home-canned low-acid foods** (vegetables, meats, soups, sauces — anything
  requiring pressure canning)
- **Wild-harvested (foraged) mushrooms**
- **Raw or undercooked meat, poultry, or seafood preparations** intended to be
  eaten as such (e.g., home-cured charcuterie, raw-milk cheeses, sushi from
  uninspected fish)
- **Alcoholic beverages** (home-brew, infusions, or otherwise)
- Any food from an animal you slaughtered outside an inspected facility

This list may be updated as laws or safety guidance change. The in-app dish
editor, the moderation system, and the "Prohibited food" report reason all
enforce this section.

## 6. Honest disclosure is an obligation, not a courtesy

If you offer food, you **must**:

- declare every major allergen you know or reasonably should know it contains
  (including cross-contamination — e.g. "prepared in a kitchen that handles
  nuts");
- describe the dish accurately (ingredients, preparation, storage);
- disclose anything a reasonable eater would want to know before eating it.

**Knowingly misdeclaring allergens or ingredients is a bannable offense** and
may expose you to personal liability to the person you harmed.

user report, pending review. We are not obligated to monitor trades but may
review reported content.
