# VillageFeed Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 6, 2026_

VillageFeed ("the app") helps neighbors trade portions of home-cooked meals.
This policy describes what we collect and how it's used.

## What we collect

- **Account**: your email address (or Google account email) for sign-in.
- **Profile**: the name, neighborhood, bio, photos, dishes, and dietary
  preferences you choose to share. Dietary preferences may reveal health or
  religious information — share only what you're comfortable showing other
  users; they appear on your public card.
- **Activity**: swipes, matches, group membership, group messages, blocks,
  and reports.

We do **not** collect your precise location. Neighborhood is a free-text label
you type yourself.

## How it's used

- Profiles, dishes, and dietary pills are shown to other signed-in users so
  they can decide whether to trade with you.
- Group messages are visible only to members of that group.
- Swipes are private; "who liked you" identities are visible only to the other
  party after a mutual match, or to VillageFeed Plus subscribers as a count of
  and list of people who liked them.
- **Moderation**: when a profile is reported or newly submitted, its text
  content is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for automated safety review, and
  may also be read by a human moderator. Reports freeze the reported profile
  immediately pending review.

## Sharing

We don't sell your data or show ads. Data is processed by our infrastructure
providers: Supabase (database, auth, storage — including Google sign-in
brokering) and Anthropic (automated moderation of profile text only). Photos
you upload are stored in a bucket that is publicly readable by URL — don't
upload photos you wouldn't show a neighbor.

## Retention & deletion

Delete your account any time in **Profile → Account → Delete account**. This
permanently removes your profile, dishes, photos, swipes, matches, group
memberships, and messages from our servers. Local app data on your device is
wiped at the same time.

## Payments

VillageFeed Plus is billed through Apple. We never see your payment details.

## Children

VillageFeed is for users 17 and older.
